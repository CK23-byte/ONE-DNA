Certifications & Compliance

Third-party verified credentials for ONE-DNA™ products

Environmental

EPD Certification

Environmental Product Declaration providing transparent, third-party verified data on environmental impact throughout the product lifecycle.

  • Scope: Cradle-to-gate with end-of-life options
  • Standard: ISO 14025, EN 15804
  • Validity: 5 years from issue date
Request EPD
Safety

EN 71-3 Compliance

Certified compliance with European toy safety standards for migration of certain elements. Ensures safe use in environments with children.

  • Scope: Heavy metal migration testing
  • Standard: EN 71-3:2019
  • Application: Schools, playgrounds, residential
Request Certificate
Chemical

REACH Compliance

Full compliance with EU REACH regulation for chemical substances. No SVHC (Substances of Very High Concern) above threshold.

  • Scope: All product components
  • Standard: EC 1907/2006
  • PFAS: PFAS-free formulation
Request Declaration
Recyclability

SGS Intron Testing

Independent verification of recyclability by SGS Intron. Confirms compatibility with existing PE recycling processes.

  • Scope: Material recyclability assessment
  • Testing: Melt flow, contamination, processing
  • Result: Suitable for PE recycling streams
