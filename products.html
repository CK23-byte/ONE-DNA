ONE-DNA™ Product Range

Professional-grade mono-material artificial grass systems. 100% PE. Designed for circularity.

Recreation & Schools

ONE-DNA™ Play 24

Versatile 24mm pile height system ideal for recreational play areas, schoolyards, and multi-use facilities. Durable, safe, and 100% mono-material.

Key Features

  • Pile Height: 24mm - optimal for play and recreation
  • Material: 100% Polyethylene (PE) - fibers, backing, and coating
  • Safety: EN 71-3 certified for child safety
  • Durability: UV stabilized, weather resistant
  • Maintenance: Low maintenance requirements
  • End-of-life: Take-back program eligible

Ideal Applications

Schoolyards Playgrounds Parks Residential Multi-use areas
