Circularity & Take-back

From installation to recycling. How ONE-DNA™ closes the material loop.

The Challenge with Traditional Artificial Grass

Traditional artificial grass consists of multiple materials bonded together: polypropylene backing, latex adhesives, polyurethane coatings, and various fiber types. This multi-material construction makes recycling technically impossible without expensive separation processes that often aren't economically viable.

The result: millions of square meters of artificial grass end up in landfill or incineration each year. Even when recycled, it's typically "downcycled" into low-value applications, losing most of its material value.

The ONE-DNA™ Take-back Program

Designed for circularity from the start

Mono-Material Design

100% polyethylene construction - fibers, backing, and coating are all PE. No material separation needed at end-of-life.

Verified Take-back

Every ONE-DNA™ installation is eligible for our take-back program. We collect, process, and return materials to the cycle.

Traceable Chain

Full traceability from installation to recycling. Documentation available for sustainability reporting.

How It Works

The ONE-DNA™ circular journey

1

Design

ONE-DNA™ is engineered from 100% polyethylene. All components - fibers, backing, and coating - are PE-based, ensuring material uniformity.

2

Installation

Professional installation by certified partners. Every installation is registered in our system for end-of-life management.

3

Use Phase

Typical service life of 8-15 years depending on application and maintenance. Full support throughout the lifecycle.

4

Collection

At end-of-life, certified collection partners remove the field. Material is transported to approved processing facilities.

5

Processing

Because ONE-DNA™ is mono-material, no separation is needed. Material is cleaned, shredded, and processed into PE pellets.

6

New Material

Recycled PE enters the material stream for new applications. True circular economy in action.

Transparency & Limitations

What the Program Enables

  • Guaranteed collection at end-of-life via partner network
  • Verified processing at approved facilities
  • Documentation for sustainability reporting (CSRD, EPR)
  • Material tracking throughout the chain

Important Considerations

  • Take-back availability varies by region - contact us for local coverage
  • Processing infrastructure depends on local capabilities
  • Recycled content percentage in new products varies
  • Collection must be coordinated with certified partners

* Circularity is a system outcome. ONE-DNA™ provides the technical foundation (mono-material design) and infrastructure (take-back network), but actual recycling depends on correct implementation and regional capabilities. We are transparent about these dependencies.
