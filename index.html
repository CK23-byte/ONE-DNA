The Multi-Material Problem

Traditional artificial grass consists of multiple materials: PP backing, latex adhesives, PU coatings, and various fiber types.

  • Impossible to separate at end-of-life
  • Ends up in landfill or incineration
  • No viable recycling pathway exists
  • Contributes to linear economy waste

The ONE-DNA™ Solution

ONE-DNA™ is engineered from a single material: polyethylene (PE). Fibers, backing, and coating are all PE-based.

  • No material separation needed
  • Compatible with existing PE recycling
  • Verified take-back infrastructure
  • Enables true circular material flow
